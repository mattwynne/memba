# Problems

## Club homepage has no navigation to Memba or other clubs

Observed: 2026-06-07

When someone is on a club homepage, there is no obvious way to navigate back to the main Memba homepage or to their other clubs. This can leave signed-in members stuck in one club-specific context when they want to return to Memba or switch club context.

Expected:

- Club homepages should provide a clear way to navigate back to the Memba homepage.
- Signed-in members should have a clear way to reach their other clubs from a club homepage.
- Visitors and members should not need to rely on browser history or manually editing the URL to move between Memba and club contexts.
