# Problems

## Spammers could abuse open signups to send unwanted email through Memba

Observed: 2026-06-05

If Memba opens public signups, bad actors may create accounts and use the product to send unsolicited or abusive email. This could harm Memba’s sender reputation, reduce deliverability for legitimate clubs and members, and put essential product email at risk.

Expected:

- Genuine users can try Memba without exposing other users to spam or abuse.
- Memba protects its email sender reputation and keeps legitimate messages deliverable.
- New or untrusted accounts cannot easily send high-volume, unsolicited, or abusive email.
