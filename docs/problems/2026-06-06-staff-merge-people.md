## Staff need to be able to merge people

Observed: 2026-06-06

Status: Partially addressed by iteration 070. Its console-only first slice merges verified email
addresses and overlapping ordinary memberships while preserving audit/history; transfers involving
unique memberships, roles, or custom-group participation remain unresolved.

Staff have no recorded way to combine two person records when they identify that both records refer to the same real person.

Expected:

- Staff can resolve duplicate person records by merging them.
- The resulting person record preserves the relevant membership and contact information needed to support the person.
