# Problems

## The interface feels too visually fancy instead of simple and app-like

Observed: 2026-06-23

Status: Unresolved.

The current product design feels too fancy for the way members are likely to use Memba, especially on a phone. On mobile, the experience should feel simple, clean, and app-like, closer to the focused feel of Topicbox than a decorative marketing site.

This is related to earlier design-alignment notes, but distinct: the problem is not one specific wireframe diverging from the app. It is a broader product design direction for member-facing screens across phone and desktop.

Expected:

- On a phone, member-facing screens feel simple, clean, and app-like.
- On a desktop, the interface stays simple while making good use of the larger screen.
- The product establishes clear information-architecture patterns so members can understand where they are and what to do next.
