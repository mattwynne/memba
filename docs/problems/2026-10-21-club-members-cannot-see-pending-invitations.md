# Problems

## Club members cannot see pending invitations

Observed: 2026-10-21

As a club member, you cannot see the club's pending invitations.

Expected:

- Club members should be able to see which invitations are still pending.
- Members should be able to distinguish pending invitations from accepted membership.

Related: [Pending invitations cannot be managed](2026-06-08-pending-invitations-cannot-be-managed.md) covers Staff and Membership Admin management actions. This observation concerns visibility for club members, not permission to resend or cancel invitations.
