### 2. Zeffy Deep-Dive: Is "Free" a Threat?

Zeffy is the most significant **pricing-model threat** to Memba.

* **Sustainability:** They are a B Corp that relies entirely on voluntary tips from donors at checkout. Their model is highly effective for one-off fundraising but less so for recurring membership dues.
* **The Catch:** While it is "free," it is **transactional**. If an outdoor club uses it for dues, the donor is asked to tip Zeffy on every transaction. This can frustrate members if used for routine annual dues, and it provides no CRM depth.
* **Gap vs. Memba:** Zeffy **does not** manage complex membership state (lapsed/active cycles, multi-family profiles, age-up transitions). It lacks a member-only portal, private content, and trip/event management logic.
* **Assessment:** Zeffy is not an AMS replacement. It is a "payment collection" tool. It erodes the "lowest price" argument but actually **strengthens** the need for a domain-model-specific tool like Memba. An organization choosing Zeffy still has to "stitch together" a directory, communication, and event management elsewhere.

---

### 3. Emerging-Entrants Briefing

The market is shifting away from "all-in-one" towards **"community-first"** and **"automated workflow"** tools.

* **Heylo:** Currently the most disruptive "new" player. It is mobile-first, focuses on group chat/events, and handles waivers well for a social-focused app. It is gaining traction in hobbyist groups (running/cycling clubs) that find legacy AMS "too boring."
* **Civic Champs:** While focused on volunteers, their move toward "consumer-app design" makes them a threat to the *volunteer-admin usability* hypothesis.
* **Activity Messenger:** Rising as a high-automation alternative for sports/activity clubs, specifically targeting the "simplicity" gap left by complex legacy software like SportLoMo.

---

### 4. Canadian & Commonwealth Market Note

* **Canada:** **Amilia** is the regional heavyweight. They are highly professional, Canadian-based, and solve the "family" and "waiver" problem natively. If a club is large enough to have a budget, they likely use or look at Amilia.
* **UK/Ireland:** **Clubforce** and **membermojo** dominate the volunteer-club space. **membermojo** is the "simple" benchmark (cheap, no-fuss), while **Clubforce** is the "integrated" benchmark (payments + comms).
* **Surcharge Sensitivity:** High in all regions. Clubs are highly sensitive to "platform fees" on top of credit card fees. A model that hides fees or takes a % of total revenue is generally disliked by volunteer boards.

---

### 5. Revised Threat Ranking

1. **Amilia (Long-term market threat):** They are professional, Canadian-friendly, and already solve household/waiver/registration workflows. They are the benchmark for a "grown-up" version of what you are building.
2. **TidyHQ (Beachhead threat):** They are explicitly built for volunteer organizations and have deep workflow logic. They are often the tool clubs settle on when Wild Apricot is too expensive.
3. **Heylo (Beachhead threat):** Their focus on social, mobile, and "fun" community management is perfect for activity clubs. They are a direct competitor for your "club" beachhead.
4. **Zeffy (Pricing-model watch-list):** Disruptive to your pricing promise but technically limited.
5. **Legacy AMS (Wild Apricot, ClubExpress):** Stagnant, but hold high inertia.

---

### 6. Differentiation Impact

* **"Fair Pricing" Wedge:** **Weakened.** Zeffy and ticket-only tools make it hard to compete on price alone. You must frame "fair pricing" not just as "cheaper," but as "transparent (no hidden percentages) and non-extractive (we don't ask your members to tip us)."
* **Domain Model Wedge:** **Strengthened.** The existence of these tools proves the market is fragmented. No one is perfectly balancing **household-first** (the family unit) with **activity-depth** (waivers, trip leads, waitlists).
* **Strategic Pivot:** Stop trying to win on "price" and start winning on the **"Volunteer-to-Volunteer Handoff."** Legacy tools are built for "Professional Admins." If Memba is the only tool that makes it *easy for a new, non-technical volunteer to take over the club treasury/membership without a manual*, you win the niche.
