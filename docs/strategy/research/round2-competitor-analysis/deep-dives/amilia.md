# Amilia / SmartRec Deep-Dive (verification)

**Bottom line up front:** The two prior reports were both partly right. Amilia is a serious, well-funded, PE-backed scale-up — but its product gravity, pricing floor, and target customers point firmly at facilities, Parks & Rec, YMCAs and small *businesses*, not 30-300-member volunteer outdoor clubs. It is a **long-term / adjacent-market threat, not a beachhead threat.**

## 1. Funding & ownership history

The May 2025 raise is confirmed: **US$35M growth round announced May 8, 2025, led by private-equity firm Vertu Capital**, which invested **$30.5M on its own**, with returning backers Export Development Canada (EDC), the Canadian Business Growth Fund (CBGF), and Investissement Québec (IQ). Vertu's Eric Kafka joined the board, and the round **included a secondary component** (i.e., partial cash-out for earlier shareholders) ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/amilia-raises-35-million-led-by-vertu-capital-to-help-more-community-centres-manage-operations/); [Amilia newsroom](https://www.amilia.com/newsroom/35m); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/amilia-raises-35-million-in-growth-funding-to-accelerate-innovation-in-the-recreation--membership-management-industry-302449897.html)).

Prior to this, Amilia raised **$30M in May 2022** from the same syndicate (CBGF, EDC, IQ), bringing **total raised to ~$65M across two disclosed rounds** ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/amilia/__0Hb1hbYGTT17-OHVO94oJPtfBxcD390rSZQp9WjOsNQ/funding-and-investors); [The SaaS News, 2022](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/amilia-raises-30-million-in-funding)). Reported **130% revenue growth since 2022**, with revenue around **$33.6M in 2024** ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/amilia-raises-35-million-led-by-vertu-capital-to-help-more-community-centres-manage-operations/); [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/amilia) — *confidence: medium, third-party estimate*).

**Ownership/incentive read:** Vertu Capital is described as a minority (not control) investor, so Amilia is not yet a portfolio buyout — but a PE firm with a board seat plus a secondary cash-out is exactly the investor-driven, return-seeking structure Memba's "bootstrapped, won't sell out / won't hike prices" positioning contrasts against. No evidence of past acquisitions *by* Amilia, and no confirmed price hikes found (*unverified*), but the percentage-of-revenue fee model (below) means Amilia's revenue scales automatically with customer volume — a structural pressure independent of headline price changes.

## 2. Target customer reality

Amilia's own materials and the funding coverage consistently name the same segments: **YMCAs, JCCs, Parks & Recreation agencies, community centers, camps, afterschool programs, swim schools, gymnastics/dance/martial-arts studios, and small fitness businesses** — *"over 1,500 organizations across 6,600 locations,"* processing *~$1B/year* for *1M+ participants* ([SmartRec product page](https://www.amilia.com/products/smartrec); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/amilia-raises-35-million-led-by-vertu-capital-to-help-more-community-centres-manage-operations/)).

There is **no dedicated "Amilia for Clubs" product** aimed at volunteer-run clubs. The closest is generic "membership management" and a 2024-era partnership with RecTech for camp/league management ([Amilia newsroom](https://www.amilia.com/newsroom/partnership-rectech-league-camp-management)). The center of gravity is clearly **staffed facilities and municipal/non-profit recreation**, not volunteer outdoor clubs. This validates the *facility / Parks-&-Rec-focused* report's core claim.

## 3. Pricing reality

Pricing is **publicly listed** (not contact-sales-only for entry tiers), which is unusual and useful ([Amilia pricing](https://www.amilia.com/pricing)):

- **Standard — $99/mo** (up to 5,000 accounts, 1 store, 10 facilities); implementation **from $899**
- **Advanced — $499/mo** (10,000 accounts, 3 stores, 20 facilities, 2 add-ons); implementation **from $2,999**
- **Custom — contact sales** ("fixed & growth-based pricing")

**Payment model is the real cost:** a **1% service fee on every invoice**, plus **2.75–2.85% + $0.30 credit-card** and **1% + $0.50 eCheck** processing fees ([Amilia pricing](https://www.amilia.com/pricing); [How Amilia Bills](https://help.amilia.com/en/articles/3433665-how-amilia-bills-your-organization)). The 1% applies to gross invoice value — reviewers complain it's deducted **even when clients pay cash** ([Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/198905/SmartRec/reviews/)). So even the $99 tier carries a perpetual ~1% tax on all member money flowing through, plus a four-figure setup fee.

## 4. Fit for a 30-300-member volunteer outdoor club

Poor-to-mediocre on cost and complexity. Verified Capterra reviews cite a *"complicated learning curve,"* a system *"very difficult to use"* needing **weekly support calls after six months**, and members/clients who *"find it difficult to navigate."* Multiple smaller orgs call it *"on the pricey side"* and *"expensive for what is offered"* ([Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/198905/SmartRec/reviews/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/amilia-smartrec/reviews)). Onboarding/support is generally *praised* as comprehensive — but a comprehensive, hand-held onboarding is itself a tell that this is **staffed-organization software**: a volunteer treasurer giving 2-3 hours a month is the opposite of the customer who completes a multi-week onboarding and runs weekly support calls. The $899-$2,999 setup fee alone is a hard barrier for a small club. Net: **enterprise-grade overkill** for a 30-300 member volunteer club.

## 5. Waivers / household / trip features

- **Household / family memberships: YES, genuine.** Family accounts link multiple persons (adults + children) under one household, and "family memberships" cover unlimited linked members ([Family Account help](https://help.amilia.com/en/articles/9369286-family-account); [Family Memberships help](https://help.amilia.com/en/articles/3433688-family-memberships-legacy-version)).
- **Waivers: PARTIAL.** No dedicated e-signature liability-waiver product surfaced; instead, configurable **registration forms** (up to ~200 questions: medical, emergency contact, "permissions and waivers") attached to registration ([Memberships collection](https://help.amilia.com/en/collections/2063141-memberships)). Adequate as a form, weaker as a true tracked liability-waiver workflow (*confidence: medium*).
- **Trip / activity-leader workflows: NO evidence.** Nothing about trip sign-ups, trip leaders, or outdoor-activity coordinator roles — the closest is generic activity/class registration and staff scheduling. This is the clearest product gap versus a mountaineering-club use case.

## 6. Verdict — resolving the disagreement

Amilia is **not a beachhead threat** to Memba's wedge (small volunteer outdoor/activity clubs): its pricing floor, four-figure onboarding, facility/Parks-&-Rec orientation, and absence of trip/activity-leader features mean it doesn't compete for the Kootenay Mountaineering Club type of customer today. The "#1 long-term threat / grown-up Memba" framing overstates *immediate* danger but is right about *trajectory* — Amilia is the best-capitalized, fastest-growing player in adjacent recreation membership management, and with $35M of PE growth capital it could move down-market or bolt on club features. So both reports were half-right: **treat Amilia as a long-term / adjacent-market watch-item and a strategic-narrative foil ("PE-backed, takes 1% of every dollar, won't sweat a volunteer club"), not as a competitor fighting Memba for its first 50 clubs.**
