# Membee (membee.com) — brief competitor note

Category: **legacy/association management system (AMS)**, alongside Wild Apricot and
ClubExpress. Adjacent to Memba, not a head-on competitor.

- Checked: 2026-05-29, marketing homepage only (not a verified feature audit).

## What it is

AMS pitched at **staffed membership organizations** — professional/trade
associations and **chambers of commerce**. Tagline: "Grow Your Organization, Don't
Administrate It!" North American client base; no public pricing.

Two tiers: **Membee Complete** (hosted drag-and-drop website builder included) and
**Membee** (integrates with an existing website).

## Features (from marketing pages)

Automated onboarding/renewals, event registration with custom pricing, member
self-service profiles, public/private directories, members-only content gating,
email/CRM, financial reporting (60+ reports), 8 payment gateways.

Markets a **flat monthly subscription that doesn't rise with growth/usage** — the
same anti-Wild-Apricot pricing message Memba uses. Worth noting a competitor already
claims that ground (for associations).

## Relevance to Memba

Weak fit for the volunteer-outdoor-club beachhead:

- Built for *staffed* associations/chambers, not *volunteer-run* clubs — different
  buyer, complexity, and price tolerance.
- No public sign of households/families, waivers/emergency contacts, volunteer-led
  trip/activity workflows, or volunteer-to-volunteer handoff. (Absent from public
  pages, not proven absent.)

Treat as a legacy-AMS benchmark to be aware of — especially its flat-pricing claim —
not a competitor Memba would meet in a deal for a 30–300-member club.
