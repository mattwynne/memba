# KMC email thread — primary-source signal (2026-05)

Source: private KMC (Kootenay Mountaineering Club) email discussion.
Thread: https://app.fastmail.com/mail/Inbox/A0mAFC83maFw.Stoj_6cm3G1k?u=360641ae

A real, unprompted discussion among members of a volunteer-run outdoor club, hitting
the exact problems Memba targets and naming the BCMC events page (see `bcmc.md`) as
the model they wish they had.

## Problems noted in the thread

- **Email/list notifications are failing.** Members aren't reliably receiving trip
  notices; some haven't received notifications in months. The mailing-list tool
  (Topicbox) is unreliable.
- **No browsable, persistent trip schedule.** Trips live in transient emails rather
  than a website members can browse any time.
- **Late-joiners miss trips.** A member who joins after a trip is announced never
  sees the announcement, so they can't sign up.
- **Word-of-mouth is doing the work the system should do.** Most attendees heard
  about a recent event in person, not through the club's tooling.
- **Big trips need long lead time.** Larger mountaineering trips need 1–2 months'
  advance promotion; the current ad-hoc, pop-up approach doesn't give members with
  jobs and kids enough notice.
- **Not enough trip leaders / posted trips**, especially for mid-level (e.g. C3)
  trips; suggestions included a co-leader system and a monthly automated "upcoming
  trips" digest.
- **Member retention risk.** Younger members are questioning the club's value and
  may drift away without a fuller, posted trip schedule.

## What the thread points to (their stated ideal)

The BCMC events page model: detailed trip descriptions, sign up whenever you read
about a trip, advance planning for busy people, and browse-by-interest rather than
receiving every trip by email.

## Corroboration for the BCMC note

A participant who was on the BCMC executive confirmed the **BCMC site is fully
custom, built ~2012, and still needs a volunteer team to maintain** — direct,
unprompted validation of the prior-art thesis in `bcmc.md`.

- Primary-source problem validation (failing notifications, no persistent schedule,
