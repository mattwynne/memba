Proposed form-wide meaning: Save anyway saves your edits, with explicit confirmation for the listed conflicts. Cancel my changes discards the whole draft, closes the editor and shows current saved data. It does not discard just the conflicting field.

Fields you have not changed should retain their latest saved values, not be overwritten by an old form snapshot. This is a proposed rule for future multi-field forms, not yet a technical design or an agreed expansion of 068. Phone numbers remain out of scope.
