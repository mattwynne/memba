# Manual demo script: staff area redesign

Use this after implementation of `docs/iterations/021-staff-area-redesign/plan.md`.

1. Sign in as Memba staff.
2. Confirm the staff shell feels like the supplied operations mockups: clear header, dense but readable tables, calm status styling, and consistent page structure.
3. Confirm staff navigation shows working links only: Clubs, People, Messages, Deliveries.
4. Confirm staff navigation does not show Incoming or Roles.
5. Open Clubs.
6. Confirm existing club creation still works.
7. Open a club.
8. Confirm club facts/editing, people records, and memberships are visually distinct.
9. Confirm the page does not offer staff a way to send a club message.
10. Confirm existing person creation/editing and email-address editing still work.
11. Confirm existing membership add/remove behaviour still works.
12. Open People.
13. Confirm people are shown as identity/contact records, not as one-club-only members.
14. Confirm a person with memberships in multiple clubs appears as one person with multiple memberships.
15. Open Messages.
16. Confirm messages from multiple clubs can be seen in one read-only operations list.
17. Confirm Messages does not offer New message, Resend, Delete, or unsupported filters.
18. Open a message from the Messages list.
19. Confirm existing delivery diagnostics remain visible.
20. Open Deliveries.
21. Confirm existing delivery diagnostics remain visible and no “opened” status has been reintroduced.
