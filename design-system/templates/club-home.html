We're a group of 142 folks based out of Nelson, BC, who love getting into the Selkirks and Purcells together — backcountry skiing in winter, alpine climbing and scrambling once the snow clears. There's a trip on the calendar most weekends of the year.

The club's been running since 2011, and everyone's welcome, whether you're chasing your first summit or you've been at it for decades.
