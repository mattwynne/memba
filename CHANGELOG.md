# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Developer tooling: `dev bdd pending` lists remaining domain work and `dev bdd pick` activates one exact declaration. Approved domain-focused plans can use `dev fabro deliver <plan> --domain-only` to implement scenarios one at a time with full checks, leaving review and merge separate.

- Members can change their own name in Account settings → Profile. Their current name and initials follow them across clubs and earlier contributions, and open member pages refresh automatically. Conflicting older edits warn before replacing a newer name.
